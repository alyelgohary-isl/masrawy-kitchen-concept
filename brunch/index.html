Good to know
What time is brunch at Masrawy?

Every day from 10am to 2pm, including weekdays. On the Nile is weekends only.

Do I need a reservation for brunch?

Tables for eight or more can be reserved; smaller groups are first come, first served. Weekends are the busiest.

Is there a vegan brunch option?

Yes: ful madames, tamaaya and pickled eggplant are vegan. Feteer and the egg plates are vegetarian.

What is feteer?

A flaky, many-layered Egyptian pastry. Masrawy serves it as Sweet Cleopatra, with feta mix and molasses and tahina or honey.
