What is kebda Eskandarani?

Kebda Eskandarani is Alexandria-style beef liver: thin slices seared fast with garlic, chilli, cumin and lime, eaten with bread.

$20at Masrawy
100% halalevery dish
Order for pick-upFull menu

Alexandria's street carts made this dish famous. Liver is sliced thin, seared on a flat top for under a minute with garlic, green chilli, cumin and a squeeze of lime, and stuffed into a fino roll or piled on balady bread. Done right it is tender and bright, nothing like the liver most people grew up avoiding.

The 'Eskandarani' means from Alexandria (Eskandareya), and the chilli is not optional there.

At Masrawy

Masrawy sears its kebda with chilli peppers and garlic, served with balady bread, tahina and lemon for $20 on the beef menu, or as a two-baguette sandwich for $20. Order it spicy for the full Alexandria experience.

Questions
Is kebda Eskandarani spicy?

Yes, it is cooked with chilli peppers. Ask for mild if you prefer.

What is it served with?

Balady bread, tahina and lemon on the plate; the sandwich version comes in two soft baguettes with Egyptian pickles and fries.

Is the liver halal?

Yes. All beef at Masrawy is halal, grass-fed and locally sourced.
