What is macarona bechamel?

Macarona bechamel is Egypt's baked pasta: penne layered with spiced minced beef and a thick béchamel, baked until the top is golden.

Every Egyptian family has a macarona bechamel, and every one is the best. Penne (or ziti) is layered with minced beef cooked with onion and spice, then covered in a béchamel so thick it sets when it cools, and baked until the top browns. It is the dish for Friday lunch, for guests, for the first day back home.

It is close to a Greek pastitsio, but the spicing is Egyptian (cumin, cinnamon, a little nutmeg in the sauce) and the béchamel is unapologetically generous.

At Masrawy

Masrawy's version uses penne, seasoned ground beef, béchamel and Canadian cheddar, baked and served in a handmade clay pot for $18. Add a chicken panne cutlet for $7. It has been on the menu since the restaurant opened in 2018, and it is on the corporate catering menu too.

Questions
Is macarona bechamel spicy?

No. It is rich and mild, a family dish; the kids' menu has a petite version of the chicken panne that pairs with it.

Can I order it for a group?

Yes. It is on the corporate catering menu at $22 per person with grilled eggplant and Masrawy salad, and on the private catering list by the tray.

Is there a vegetarian version?

The private catering list has zucchini bechamel; ask the team about availability.
