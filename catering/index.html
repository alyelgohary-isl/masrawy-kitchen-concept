Catering is where Masrawy started.

Before the restaurant there were four years of weddings, engagements and office lunches: Toronto's first professional Egyptian caterer, since 2014. Trays for family tables, per-person menus for the office, and a food truck for the company barbecue.

Questions
How much notice does Masrawy catering need?

48 hours for most orders; longer for weddings and large events. Changes and cancellations need 24 hours.

Is there a minimum order?

On the corporate menu, 10 of each item. Delivery needs a $300 minimum; pick-up from the restaurant is free with no minimum.

Do you deliver across the GTA?

Yes. Delivery fees start at $10 and depend on location; allow a 15-minute window either side of the requested time.

Can you handle vegan, vegetarian or gluten-free guests?

Yes. Ask for a custom menu; koshary, tamaaya, ful, mahshi and most dips are vegan.

Is the food halal?

Everything Masrawy cooks is 100% halal.
